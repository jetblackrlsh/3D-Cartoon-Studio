Characters and animation presets

Fast Mode role for selected actor

Changing role does not delete animation or appearance. Full 3D is the safe override. For JSON-authored story roles, use speaking, handlesObjects, combat, or plotRelevant=true to protect actors independently.

Applying replaces only the selected actor’s animation in this scene. It starts at the scene beginning; authored actions return to idle and captured clips hold their final pose. Captured skating clips are excluded from this public edition. Appearance changes do not change the source motion. Add music, action sounds and interaction targets separately.

Reusable 3D visual effects

All 22 effects use deterministic 3D geometry. These are visual effects, not sound effects. Position and timing are editable in the scene JSON; actor/socket attachments and 3-axis rotation are also supported.

Reusable 3D props

volumetric catalog props. Position becomes a socket offset when attached. Edit props JSON for palette channels, three-axis rotation, visibility intervals, movement and ownership transfers. Props are scene geometry, not physics objects; review grips and two-handed contact for each shot.

Reusable 3D environments

The catalog sets are procedural 3D scenery, not backdrop images. Actors and props are retained. Interiors are open-front cutaways. Water sets need appropriate swimming or boat staging; environments do not provide physics collision. Edit environment options in JSON to recolor or hide terrain, landmarks, furnishings or atmosphere.

Projects & export

Fast Mode uses a smaller picture and intentionally choppy 6 fps motion. Only explicitly marked non-interacting background extras become cheap flat cutouts; heroes, speakers, object handlers, combatants and plot-relevant actors stay full 3D. Cameras and depth placement still matter. No shadow or anti-aliasing quality promise is made for the CPU exporter.

New Fast projects start with two varied original-rig cast presets. Existing imported characters keep their identity and appearance; no automatic rig substitution. The original library remains diverse in genre, skin tone, hair and outfit.

All artwork, music and generated speech travel with the project. Edits are kept in memory until you save. Preview is WebGL when supported, with a CPU fallback.

Browser recording runs in real time and requires keeping the tab visible. Output is MP4 if supported, otherwise WebM. The included headless renderer produces MP4 independently of browser recording.

Character & action reference

Choose generic procedural people, woodland animals, an editable stock cast, or a genuine imported standard humanoid. The authored preset catalog contains 58 actions. Captured skating clips are not distributed. Use motion templates, editable actor JSON, props, effects and camera tracks for reusable staging.

Actor rotation is in radians. Shot and action times are normalized 0–1. Headless API: CartoonStudio.validate, timeline, locate, stage, update, SoftwareRenderer.

Scene editor

Traditional synthetic speech is generated locally. It does not clone anyone’s voice. Higher-quality recordings can be imported without changing the animation.

Fresh stills

Capture a movie moment, or change the copied scene JSON to compose a fresh pose and camera. Your original movie stays unchanged. No transition fade is applied.

Edit fresh scene: camera / target / actors / props / shots

To use the camera and target directly, set shots to an empty array. Actor rotation is in radians; normalized animation times are 0–1. Copy a scene first, then adjust its values.

Comic pages

Capture current scenes as independent panel snapshots. Compose a page, edit its captions and bubbles, and export an editable project or a multi-page PDF. All assets stay embedded. Changes are in memory until saved.

Bubble and caption guide

A panel can have caption text and a balloons array. Example: {"kind":"speech","text":"Hello!","x":0.05,"y":0.05,"w":0.65,"h":0.25,"tail":[0.7,0.5],"fontSize":22}. Coordinates are fractions of the panel. Kinds: speech, thought, caption. Optional text controls: allCaps, strokeColor, strokeWidth, color, fill. Oversized text causes an explicit export error instead of being silently cut off. Use panel sceneOverride to change its pose without changing the movie. Cover and credits pages can also have a text field.
